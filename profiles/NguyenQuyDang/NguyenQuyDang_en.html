Nguyen Quy Dang is an M.Sc. student in Computer Science at MBZUAI, and a researcher at AVITECH. He received his B.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence from VNU University of Engineering and Technology in December 2025.

His research interests include high-performance computing, systems for machine learning, and machine learning for systems. His research also spans tensor decomposition, streaming algorithms, and federated learning. He is the author of RE-LL1 and Fast Robust Triple Tensor Decomposition With Data Corruption, both published at ICASSP 2026.
