• Research Member at AVITECH Research Group

Nguyen Phuong Nam is currently a research member of the AVITECH Research Group. His research focuses on longitudinal data analysis and multimodal deep learning for modeling and predicting the progression of Alzheimer's disease over time. His work aims to leverage multi-timepoint and multimodal biomedical data for early detection, cognitive decline prediction, and disease conversion assessment.
